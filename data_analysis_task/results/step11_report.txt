5. ML READINESS
   Safe to train on (real label, news page, confidence >= 0.6, not flagged): 71,674 of 95,094 (75.4%)
   Per category: market_stocks 5,230, technology 16,587, health 4,888, energy 724, politics 13,549, other 30,696
   Class balance: largest class is 42x the smallest. Use class weights or sampling.
   Duplicate titles: 589 articles share a title with another article (234 groups); 27 groups got different labels.
   After keeping one article per title: 71,319 training candidates (saved in ml_training_candidates.csv).
   Shortcut check: a model that only knows the DOMAIN gets 67.6% right (always guessing 'other' gets 42.7%).
   A text model is only impressive if it clearly beats the domain-only number.
   Split train/test by domain or by time, so the model cannot just recognise a site.

6. HONEST WARNINGS FOR THE ML STAGE
   a) The labels come from keyword rules. A model trained on them mostly relearns those rules.
   b) Judge any model on the hand-labelled articles (300 in total), never on rule labels.
   c) Rule accuracy was 68.5% (v1.2, 200 articles) and 73.0% (v1.3, 100 articles); expect label noise.
   d) Use only the 'safe to train on' rows, or give the flagged ones lower weight.
